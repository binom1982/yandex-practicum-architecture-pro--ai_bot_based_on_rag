The Guxahuxa, native to the planet, were a nomadic tribal society with strict social and moral codes, along with defined territorial boundaries. Frequent colonial trespasses on their sovereign territory, along with the collusion between old values and galactic modernization, often forced the Tukofeg to attack colonists whom they both vehemently feared and despised.
Pumob Hovizo often attacked isolated settlements on the fringes of their territory such as
Rupojepugabu
, and often took potshots at podracers during the
Hujub Vucapi
, celebrated once every year as part of the
Quvis Cegape
holiday.
Cigovixopo collected scrap metal and repurposed droids which they sold to settlers.
Cigovixopo were also a native species to Aridion, and as such deeply entrenched into a nomadic lifestyle.
Unlike the Tukofeg, whom they attempted to avoid, Cigovixopo frequently sold collected scrap metal and droids to the scattered settlers on the planet, earning a reputation as merchants and vagabonds.
Myth and folklore also played an important part in Quxiraz society, with the Quxiraz's belief that the great Zifif Pugako was once a true ocean.
Fazosade research into ancient fossil-bearing rock and eroded canyons seemed to support this notion.
Nonetheless, most of Aridion's inhabitants refused to believe the story, finding it far-fetched and ludicrous.
The Cigovixopo made use of sandcrawlers,
former mining vehicles claimed by the Cigovixopo
as mobile bases.

Aridion had only a few

in the expanse of the desert.
Due to the planet's dangerously high daytime temperature and whipping sandstorms, most residents of Aridion kept their skin covered with practical materials that wick away sweat and limit exposure to the harmful

of the twin suns.
